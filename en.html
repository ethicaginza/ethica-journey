Philosophy

Our aim: a journey through time on a plate.

The menu lists no dish names, only the names of eras such as “Birth of the Earth” and “The Age of Dinosaurs and the Great Extinction.” What appears is revealed only when the plate arrives before you. Landscapes from the dawn of the Earth, and the wisdom, techniques and spices nurtured by each civilization, are reimagined with modern techniques and woven into a single story across the course. We hope the hours you spend here linger as a memory that goes beyond a meal.

Each dish distills a fragment of time and space into a journey for all five senses.

  • FlavorPeruvian cuisine as the foundation, layered with French technique. Above all, we value harmony of flavor and a single, clear thread running from the first dish to the last. Even when a flavor is new to you, the aroma of spice and honest umami come through clearly.
  • TimeThe ocean of 3.8 billion years ago, the age of dinosaurs, trade across the seas. Each dish captures a scene from a bygone era.
  • LandscapeThe stillness of the deep sea, the pulse of the land. We paint each era's landscape onto the plate.
  • Tableware & SoundThe journey begins with sound enveloping the room, and with each new chapter the tableware changes, with pieces commissioned from potters across Japan.

Cenote

The deeper you dive, the further back in time.

A cenote, the sacred spring of ancient Latin American civilizations, has suddenly appeared in Ginza.

The deeper the earth, the older its layers. At the bottom of a cenote, stalactites formed in the Ice Age still lie beneath the water.

Our blue tables are its surface. With each dish, you dive to the depth of a different era. The first takes you to the ocean of 3.8 billion years ago; the third, to the deepest point of all, 4.6 billion years ago. From there you rise, then dive deep once more at the end. A journey in eight chapters.

Executive Chef

Cuisine as a language of land and history.

Executive chef Matsunae trained at two-Michelin-star MAZ. Building on flavors honed in Peruvian cuisine, he paints the history and memory of a place onto the plate. Here in Ginza, he pursues dishes that reflect even the memory of the Earth and the history of civilizations.

MASAO ANDRÉS MATSUNAE GUZMÁN

Beyond MAZ (two Michelin stars, innovative) and Torahou (one Michelin star, Chinese), he has gained experience in Japanese, French, Italian and Spanish kitchens to create a cuisine all his own.

Q&A

Before you book.

It's my first visit. Which course should I choose?

We recommend the ETHICA Collection (8 chapters), which follows the full ETHICA story. If you have plans afterward, the 5-chapter Short Course is also available.

How long does the meal take?

About 1.5 hours for the Short Course (5 chapters), about 2.5 hours for the ETHICA Collection (8 chapters), and 2.5–3 hours for the 9-chapter courses.

Can you help us celebrate an anniversary?

We can prepare a hand-painted message plate. Please choose the Birthday & Anniversary Course, or let us know when you book. A Ginza Calon "Everlasting Bouquet" is also available on request.

Do you have a private room for business dinners?

There is a private room inside the kitchen for 2–4 guests, one party per day. Please book the Gastronomy Experience (9 chapters).

Can I enjoy the meal without drinking alcohol?

We offer a non-alcoholic pairing (9,800) matched to the dishes. Please let us know when you book.

Can you accommodate allergies and vegetarian diets?

Please tell us about any allergies, including those of your companions, when booking. We also offer the Vegetarian ETHICA Collection. Vegan and Muslim-friendly requests can be accommodated if you let us know when booking.

Can I book for one person?

Yes. The Short Course, ETHICA Collection, Grand Collection, Vegetarian ETHICA Collection and Chef's Counter can be booked from one guest. The Birthday & Anniversary Course and the private-room Gastronomy Experience require a minimum of two guests.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual, please. We ask that you avoid tank tops, shorts and beach sandals—otherwise, please feel at ease.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards (VISA, Mastercard, JCB, AMEX, Diners), as well as transit IC cards, iD and QUICPay.

Can I book at short notice?

Yes—if seats are available, you can book even at the last minute. Please check availability first.

Can we book the entire restaurant for a large group?

Private buyouts for up to 20 guests can be arranged (please ask about standing receptions). Please call 03-6228-7313 (12:00–17:00, Japanese only).
