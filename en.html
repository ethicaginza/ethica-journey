Philosophy

Our goal: a journey through time, on a plate.

The menu shows no dish names, only times in history, like “Birth of the Earth” or “The Age of Dinosaurs and Their Extinction.” You find out what each one is when it arrives in front of you. We take scenes from the early Earth, and the skills and spices of past cultures, and cook them with modern methods into one story. We hope your time here stays with you as more than just a meal.

Each dish turns a moment in time and place into a journey for all five senses.

  • FlavorPeruvian food is the base of our flavors. Most of all, we care about balance, and one story from the first dish to the last. Even new flavors are easy to enjoy: the smell of spices, a clean, savory taste (umami).
  • TimeThe ocean of 3.8 billion years ago, the age of dinosaurs, trade across the seas. Each dish shows a scene from the past.
  • LandscapeThe quiet of the deep sea, the energy of the land. We paint the landscape of each time onto the plate.
  • Tableware & SoundThe journey starts when music fills the room. With each new chapter, the plates and bowls change. Potters across Japan made them for us.

Cenote

The deeper you dive, the further back in time.

A cenote (a natural pool in a cave), the sacred spring of ancient Latin American peoples, has appeared in Ginza.

The deeper you go, the older the rock. At the bottom of a cenote, stalactites (stone shapes like icicles) from the Ice Age are still under the water.

Our blue tables are the water's surface. Each dish takes you down to a different time. The first dish takes you to the ocean of 3.8 billion years ago. The third takes you to the deepest point: 4.6 billion years ago. Then you come back up, and dive deep once more at the end. A journey in eight chapters.

  1. 1Dish

    Dish 1:3.8 billion years ago

    Birth of the Oceans · Hot Springs on the Sea Floor

    Life may have started at hot springs on the deep sea floor. The journey begins with autumn bonito (a fish like tuna).

  2. 5Dish

    Dish 5:200 million years ago

    The Age of Dinosaurs and Their Extinction

    The age of the dinosaurs, and its end. A dish of fatty sawara (Spanish mackerel).

  3. 7Dish

    Dish 7:500 years ago

    Civilization and Trade

    500 years ago, food began to travel across the oceans. Satsuma mandarins tell this story.

Innovative Plates

Like an abstract painting, each plate is art

ETHICA's dishes rebuild the history of the Earth, human cultures and natural landscapes as food.
They are not only beautiful: above all, we aim for the best possible taste.

To reach the heart of this, we value:

  • Free ideas, not tied to traditional styles of cooking
  • Originality that brings in different cultures and skills
  • Each dish made as a work of art

These guide every dish we make.

Executive Chef

Food that tells the story of a land and its history.

Executive Chef Matsunae has worked in many kinds of kitchens: MAZ (innovative, two Michelin stars), Koho (Chinese, one Michelin star), and Japanese, French, Italian and Spanish restaurants. In his own way, he brings together the skills and sense of taste he learned in each one, and above all, he aims for the best possible flavor. With free ideas from all these experiences, he turns the history of the Earth and the memory of ancient cultures into dishes like no other.

Q&A

Before you book.

It's my first visit. Which course should I choose?

We recommend the ETHICA Collection (8 chapters). It tells the full ETHICA story. If you have plans after dinner, try the 5-chapter Short Course.

How long does the meal take?

About 1.5 hours for the Short Course (5 chapters), about 2.5 hours for the ETHICA Collection (8 chapters), and 2.5–3 hours for the 9-chapter courses.

Can you help us celebrate an anniversary?

We can make a message plate, painted by hand. Please choose the Birthday & Anniversary Course, or tell us when you book. If you ask, we can also order a Ginza Calon “Everlasting Bouquet” (long-lasting flowers).

Do you have a private room for business dinners?

Our private room inside the kitchen is for 2–4 guests, one group a day. Please book the Gastronomy Experience (9 chapters).

Can I enjoy the meal if I don't drink?

We have a non-alcoholic pairing (9,800), with a drink matched to each dish. Please tell us when you book.

Can you handle allergies and vegetarian diets?

Please tell us about any allergies, including those of others in your group, when you book. We also have the Vegetarian ETHICA Collection. For vegan or Muslim-friendly meals, please tell us when you book.

Can I book for one?

Yes. You can book the Short Course, ETHICA Collection, Grand Collection, Vegetarian ETHICA Collection and Chef's Counter for one person. The Birthday & Anniversary Course and the Gastronomy Experience (private room) need at least two guests.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual, please. Please do not wear tank tops, shorts or beach sandals. Other than that, you do not need to dress up.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards (VISA, Mastercard, JCB, AMEX, Diners), as well as transit IC cards, iD and QUICPay.

Can I book at the last minute?

Yes. If there are open seats, you can book even at the last minute. Please check the booking page first.

Can we book the whole restaurant for a large group?

You can book the whole restaurant for up to 20 guests (please ask about standing parties). Please call 03-6228-7313 (12:00–17:00, Japanese only).
